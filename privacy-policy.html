Mama Yum Yum ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through mamayummbabyfood.ca, how we use it, and your choices.

Information We Collect

When you contact us, sign up for our newsletter, place an order, or create an account, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, delivery address, and order details.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to fulfill orders, respond to inquiries, send updates you've opted into (like our newsletter), and improve our products and service.

Payment Information

Orders are placed and paid for through our online store at mamayumyumbabyfood.myshopify.com, powered by Shopify. Shopify handles payment processing securely; we do not store your full payment card details.

Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it with service providers who help us operate our business (such as payment processors, email marketing platforms, or delivery partners), solely to provide our services to you.

Your Choices

You can unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time, and can contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information.
