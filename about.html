Meet Michelle

Our Story

Where it began

Mama Yum Yum started the way most good things do — in a home kitchen, between nap times and grocery runs. Michelle was mixing up the very first purees for her own three kids, wanting to know exactly what went into every bowl. It didn't take long before other Niagara families started asking for a jar of their own.

The yum behind it

Every blend begins with real, seasonal produce — no fillers, no added sugar, nothing hidden. Small batches are cooked gently, then flash-frozen at peak freshness, so the flavour (and the nutrition) stays locked in from our kitchen to yours.

What we believe

We believe first foods should taste like real food. That feeding a baby well doesn't have to mean doing it alone, or doing it perfectly — just doing it with care. And that the best meals, tiny or otherwise, are made to be shared.

Safety & Quality

Trust, In Every Jar

Every batch is prepared to Niagara Region Public Health standards, in a strictly sanitized kitchen, and flash-frozen at peak freshness to lock in nutrients — nothing added, nothing hidden.

  • Public health compliance — certified under the Ontario Food Handler Training program.
  • Strict sanitization in every step of prep, cooking, and packaging.
  • Flash-frozen freshness to preserve flavour and nutrition, batch after batch.
