Meet Michelle

Our Story

Where It Began

Mama Yum Yum began in my very own kitchen with my second son.

There was a four-year gap between my boys, and in those years in between, I had time to grow, learn, and find my way around the kitchen. Before long, my kitchen became my safe place—a place where I could create, experiment, and pour a little love into everything I made.

When it was time to start feeding my growing boy, I began creating homemade purées just for him. And he loved them.

Mealtimes were happy, exciting, and something we both looked forward to.

But this time around, I was experiencing motherhood differently. With my first son, I simply didn’t know what I know now. I quickly realized just how stressful feeding a baby can feel when you’re unsure of what to prepare, how to prepare it, and where to even begin. And when life gets busy, it’s so easy to reach for packaged food simply because it’s convenient.

I knew there had to be another way.

So I started making my own fresh blends and sharing them with other moms through Instagram. One mama tried them, then another, and before I knew it, something that had started in my kitchen was growing into something much bigger.

And that’s how Mama Yum Yum was born.

What started as homemade purées for my own little boy became a passion for creating fresh, wholesome frozen baby food that makes mealtime easier for other mamas.

I’ve had the privilege of cooking for so many little ones and watching Mama Yum Yum grow through farmers’ markets across the region. Most importantly, I’ve seen firsthand how much a little convenience can take off a mama’s plate.

Today, I’m bringing Mama Yum Yum into the next chapter.

You can now shop online and have access to fresh, frozen baby food whenever you need it, for any meal—without the stress of preparing everything from scratch.

Because feeding your baby should be a time to nourish, connect, and enjoy those little moments together.

From my kitchen to your little one’s table, Mama Yum Yum was made with love for mamas who are doing their very best.

The Yum Behind the Highchair

There’s a lot of love that goes into every little bite.

Behind every Mama Yum Yum blend is a mama who knows that feeding a little one can be both exciting and overwhelming. It’s not just about filling a tiny tummy—it’s about introducing new flavours, new textures, and helping little ones discover their love for food.

That’s why every blend we create is made with care, keeping little taste buds and growing bodies in mind.

We believe baby food can be simple, delicious, nourishing, and convenient all at the same time. Our fresh frozen blends are prepared with thoughtfully chosen ingredients and then frozen to make mealtime easy whenever you need it.

No complicated meal prep. No wondering what to make. No standing in the kitchen trying to create the perfect baby meal when you already have a million other things to do.

Just thaw, serve, and watch that little face dig in.

And yes, sometimes there will be a little on their face, a little on their hands, and probably a little on the highchair too. That’s all part of the fun!

Because behind every spoonful of Mama Yum Yum is something bigger than baby food.

It’s about giving mamas a helping hand.

It’s about making mealtimes a little easier.

It’s about helping babies discover how delicious real food can be.

And it’s about putting a whole lot of YUM behind that highchair.

What We Believe

At Mama Yum Yum, we believe feeding your baby should feel simple, nourishing, and joyful—not stressful or overwhelming.

We believe babies deserve real food made with care, and mamas deserve a little more time, a little less pressure, and the peace of mind that comes with knowing there’s a wholesome meal ready when they need it.

We believe that convenience and quality can go hand in hand. That choosing fresh, frozen baby food doesn’t mean compromising on the love and care that goes into every meal.

We believe in keeping things simple: fresh ingredients, thoughtfully created blends, and food made with little ones in mind.

And most of all, we believe that every mama is doing her best.

Some days you have time to cook. Some days you don’t. Some days baby happily eats everything, and other days… well, that’s motherhood! 😂

Mama Yum Yum is here for all of it.

Our goal is to take one more thing off your plate, so you can spend less time worrying about what to feed your little one and more time enjoying the beautiful, messy, precious moments that come with feeding them.

Because happy babies make happy mamas—and that’s something we believe in. ❤️

Safety & Quality

Trust, In Every Jar

Every batch is prepared to Niagara Region Public Health standards, in a strictly sanitized kitchen, and flash-frozen at peak freshness to lock in nutrients — nothing added, nothing hidden.

  • Public health compliance — certified under the Ontario Food Handler Training program.
  • Strict sanitization in every step of prep, cooking, and packaging.
  • Flash-frozen freshness to preserve flavour and nutrition, batch after batch.
