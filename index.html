Meet Michelle

Our Story

"Hi Mama! I'm so glad you're here. Mama Yum Yum was born right in my own kitchen — between nap times and grocery runs, mixing up the very first purees for my own three kids. What started as a way to know exactly what was going into my babies' bowls turned into something I wanted to share with every family in Niagara."

— Michelle Winter, Founder & Mom of Three

Michelle crafts every blend from first bite to full bowl, using seasonal produce, small-batch cooking, and a whole lot of trial-and-error from her own family's high chair. Every jar is made with the same care she'd want for her own kids — because it is.

Safety & Quality

Trust, In Every Jar

Every batch is prepared to Niagara Region Public Health standards, in a strictly sanitized kitchen, and flash-frozen at peak freshness to lock in nutrients — nothing added, nothing hidden.

  • Public health compliance — certified under the Ontario Food Handler Training program.
  • Strict sanitization in every step of prep, cooking, and packaging.
  • Flash-frozen freshness to preserve flavour and nutrition, batch after batch.
